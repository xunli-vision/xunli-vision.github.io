Research introduction

I develop computer vision and applied AI methods that help intelligent systems understand the physical world. My research combines 3D vision, semantic reasoning and multimodal foundation models, with applications in robotics, digital agriculture, biodiversity, healthcare, renewable energy and transport safety.

At CSIRO, I lead the Quantitative Imaging team and multidisciplinary research collaborations with industry, universities and government. My work connects new research methods with practical sensing, analysis and decision-support systems. I lead and contribute to applied computer vision and machine learning research across robotics, digital agriculture, environmental monitoring, healthcare and renewable energy.

Research interests

  • 3D scene understanding
  • Multimodal AI and vision language foundation models
  • Human and animal behaviour understanding
  • Interpretable medical image analysis
  • AI for environmental monitoring and digital agriculture

Short biography

Dr Xun Li is a Senior Research Engineer and Team Leader of Quantitative Imaging at CSIRO Technology. Her research focuses on 3D computer vision, multimodal AI and translating research into practical systems. She leads collaborations spanning robotics, agriculture, renewable energy, biodiversity, healthcare and transport safety. She has worked across CSIRO’s Technology (formerly Data61) and Agriculture and Food business units. She previously held a postdoctoral research position at UNSW, where she also earned her PhD. She holds a Bachelor of Engineering in Software Engineering from Wuhan University.
